Industry Solutions

IoT & 5G Solutions for Healthcare

Revolutionize patient care with real-time health monitoring, predictive analytics, and connected medical devices for improved diagnostics and outcomes.

The Challenge

Healthcare Must Deliver Better Outcomes with Greater Efficiency

Hospitals and healthcare providers face mounting pressure to improve patient outcomes while controlling costs and managing staff shortages. Disconnected systems, manual processes, and reactive rather than proactive care models create inefficiencies that impact both patients and staff. Singular's IoT and private 5G platform transforms healthcare facilities into intelligent, connected environments where every device, patient, and process is seamlessly integrated.

  • Continuous patient monitoring reducing adverse events
  • Connected medical device management and integration
  • Real-time asset tracking for equipment and personnel
  • Environmental monitoring for infection control and compliance
Our Approach

Connected Care: From Device to Diagnosis to Discharge

Our healthcare IoT platform creates a seamless data flow from bedside medical devices to clinical information systems — giving clinicians real-time patient intelligence at the point of care. Private 5G provides the high-bandwidth, low-latency connectivity that next-generation medical applications demand, without compromising security or reliability.

  • Real-time integration of all medical devices and monitoring systems
  • Private 5G for high-bandwidth medical imaging and telemedicine
  • AI-powered early warning systems for patient deterioration
  • Full HIPAA/POPIA compliance with end-to-end data encryption
Our Solutions

Purpose-Built for Healthcare

We deploy a suite of integrated IoT and connectivity solutions tailored to the unique demands of healthcare operations.

Patient Monitoring IoT

Continuous monitoring of patient vital signs with AI-powered early warning algorithms that alert clinical staff to deterioration before it becomes critical — reducing adverse events and ICU admissions.

Medical Asset Tracking

Real-time location and utilization tracking of all medical equipment — infusion pumps, wheelchairs, ventilators — eliminating time wasted searching for equipment and improving asset utilization.

Private 5G Hospital Network

A dedicated, secure private 5G network for your entire hospital campus — enabling high-definition medical imaging transfer, real-time telemedicine, and connected surgical robotics.

Environmental Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of temperature, humidity, air quality, and pressure in critical areas — operating theatres, ICUs, pharmacies, and laboratories — ensuring compliance and patient safety.

Connected Medical Devices

Seamless integration of all medical devices — monitors, infusion pumps, ventilators — into a unified clinical data platform, eliminating manual charting and reducing medication errors.

Telemedicine Infrastructure

High-quality video consultation infrastructure with integrated patient records, remote monitoring data, and prescription management — extending quality care beyond the hospital walls.

Real-World Impact

Technology in Service of Better Patient Outcomes

Healthcare organisations deploying our platform consistently report improved patient safety scores, reduced length of stay, and higher staff satisfaction — through technology that supports, not replaces, the human side of care.
