Our Story

Purpose-Driven Networks for a Connected World

We are a technology company committed to redefining digital connectivity through innovative solutions that empower businesses and transform communities worldwide.

Our Mission

Empowering Global Progress through Advanced Digital Transformation

At Singular, we believe connectivity is the foundation of progress. We leverage the power of IoT, AI, and private 5G networks to drive efficiency, security, and intelligence — empowering businesses to thrive in an increasingly connected world.

With IoT at our core, we build operations that are proactive, anticipating issues before they arise, refining processes on the fly, and delivering insights in real time. This transformation redefines success across industries, from mining and manufacturing to healthcare and smart cities.

Our cutting-edge private 5G networks provide dedicated high-speed connectivity with ultra-low latency and robust security. Together, IoT and private 5G form a powerful alliance that propels your business into a future of limitless opportunities.

Our Journey

Building the Future, One Network at a Time

From our founding in Johannesburg to managing millions of endpoints globally — here's how we got here.

Founded

The Singular is Born

Founded in Sandton, Johannesburg with a clear mission: to bring purpose-driven networks and advanced IoT solutions to industries across Africa and beyond.

Early Growth

First Industry Deployments

Completed our first private 5G and IoT deployments in mining and telecommunications, proving the model and establishing our channel partner network.

Expansion
