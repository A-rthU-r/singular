Common Questions

Can't find what you're looking for? Email us directly and we'll respond within 24 hours.

What industries do you serve?

+
We serve mining, telecommunications, manufacturing, smart cities, utilities & energy, and healthcare. Our IoT and private 5G solutions are customised for the specific operational challenges and regulatory environments of each sector.

What is a private 5G network?

+
A private 5G network is a dedicated wireless network built exclusively for your organisation — not shared with the public. It provides ultra-low latency, high bandwidth, and enterprise-grade security, ideal for mission-critical industrial applications.

How do you integrate with existing infrastructure?

+
We design our solutions to integrate seamlessly with your existing systems — from legacy SCADA and OT networks to modern cloud platforms. Our team conducts a thorough discovery phase before any deployment begins.

Do you operate outside South Africa?

+
Yes. While headquartered in Johannesburg, we operate across Africa and internationally through our network of 200+ trusted channel partners worldwide.

How long does a typical deployment take?

+
Timelines vary by project scope. A typical pilot deployment runs 6–12 weeks from design to go-live. Full enterprise rollouts are planned in phases to minimise disruption to your operations.
