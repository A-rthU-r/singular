Industry Solutions

IoT & 5G Solutions for Manufacturing

Streamline production with digital twins and smart automation. Detect machinery issues before failures occur and optimize workflows in real time.

The Challenge

Modern Manufacturing Demands Real-Time Intelligence

Manufacturers face mounting pressure to improve quality, reduce waste, and increase throughput — while managing an increasingly complex mix of legacy and modern equipment. Unplanned downtime, quality defects, and inefficient workflows erode margins and competitive position. Singular's Industrial IoT platform bridges the gap between the factory floor and the boardroom.

  • Predictive maintenance eliminating unplanned production stoppages
  • Digital twin technology for process simulation and optimization
  • Real-time quality monitoring reducing scrap and rework
  • Connected workforce tools improving safety and productivity
Our Approach

Digital Twins and Smart Automation for Industry 4.0

We create digital replicas of your production lines, machines, and entire facilities — enabling real-time simulation, what-if analysis, and automated optimization. Combined with our private 5G backbone, your factory floor becomes a fully connected, intelligent production environment.

  • Digital twin modelling of entire production lines
  • Real-time OEE (Overall Equipment Effectiveness) tracking
  • AI-driven quality control and defect detection at line speed
  • Seamless integration with ERP, MES, and SCADA systems
Our Solutions

Purpose-Built for Manufacturing

We deploy a suite of integrated IoT and connectivity solutions tailored to the unique demands of manufacturing operations.

Predictive Maintenance

IoT sensors on all critical machinery continuously monitor vibration, temperature, and performance — AI models predict failures days before they occur, enabling planned maintenance.

Digital Twin Technology

Create virtual replicas of your production lines for real-time simulation, process optimization, and operator training — without stopping production.

Quality Control IoT

Computer vision and IoT sensors monitor every product at line speed, catching defects instantly and adjusting process parameters automatically to maintain quality targets.

Energy Management

Monitor and optimize energy consumption across all machines and systems — identifying waste, automating shutdown of idle equipment, and reducing your energy bill and carbon footprint.

Private 5G Factory Network

Replace unreliable Wi-Fi with a dedicated private 5G network — enabling AGVs, robotic systems, and wearables to communicate with ultra-low latency and guaranteed bandwidth.

ERP & MES Integration

Our platform integrates with your existing SAP, Oracle, Siemens, and other enterprise systems — enriching your data with real-time IoT intelligence without replacing what works.

Real-World Impact

Transform Your Factory Floor into an Intelligent Ecosystem

Manufacturers that deploy our platform consistently report significant OEE improvements, reduced scrap rates, and faster time-to-quality — without replacing existing equipment.
