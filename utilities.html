Industry Solutions

IoT & 5G Solutions for Utilities & Energy

Modernize energy distribution with advanced IoT and private 5G networks. Monitor power grids, reduce energy waste, and detect faults in real time.

The Challenge

Energy Utilities Face an Unprecedented Transition

The energy sector is undergoing its most significant transformation in a century — shifting from centralized fossil fuel generation to distributed renewable sources, while managing ageing grid infrastructure and growing consumer expectations. Singular's IoT and private 5G platform provides the intelligent foundation for the modern utility — enabling smarter grids, faster fault response, and seamless renewable integration.

  • Real-time grid monitoring and fault detection across the network
  • Renewable energy integration and distributed resource management
  • Smart metering and demand response programme management
  • Asset lifecycle management and predictive maintenance
Our Approach

Intelligent Grid Management for the Energy Transition

Our platform connects every grid asset — from transmission towers to distribution transformers, from renewable generation sites to customer smart meters — into a single intelligent network. Machine learning algorithms continuously optimize grid operation, predict demand, and detect anomalies before they become outages.

  • End-to-end grid visibility from generation to consumption
  • AI-powered load forecasting and demand response automation
  • Real-time fault detection and automated switching for faster restoration
  • SCADA integration with enriched IoT analytics layer
Our Solutions

Purpose-Built for Utilities

We deploy a suite of integrated IoT and connectivity solutions tailored to the unique demands of utilities operations.

Smart Grid Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of grid voltage, current, power quality, and load across your entire distribution network — with AI anomaly detection identifying faults before customers lose power.

Asset Management IoT

IoT sensors on transformers, switchgear, cables, and other critical assets continuously monitor health indicators — enabling predictive maintenance and extending asset lifetimes.

Renewable Integration

Intelligent monitoring and control of distributed renewable generation — solar, wind, and battery storage — enabling seamless grid integration and optimized dispatch decisions.

Smart Metering

Advanced metering infrastructure with real-time consumption data, tamper detection, and remote disconnection — enabling accurate billing, demand response, and customer engagement programmes.

Private 5G Grid Network

Dedicated private 5G communications network for your grid assets — providing the reliability, security, and low latency required for critical infrastructure control without dependence on public networks.

Outage Management

Automated outage detection, crew dispatch, and customer communication — reducing mean time to restore (MTTR) and improving regulatory compliance with outage reporting requirements.

Real-World Impact

Powering the Energy Transition with Intelligence

Utilities deploying our platform lead the transition to smarter, cleaner energy — reducing losses, improving reliability, and delivering the digital grid their customers expect.
