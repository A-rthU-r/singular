Industry Solutions

IoT & 5G Solutions for Smart Cities

Create smarter, more sustainable urban environments with intelligent infrastructure — from smart lighting and traffic management to enhanced public safety.

The Challenge

Cities Must Serve Citizens More with Less

Urban populations are growing faster than infrastructure can keep pace. City managers face the dual challenge of improving service quality while controlling costs — all under intense scrutiny from citizens and regulators. Singular's Smart City platform uses IoT, AI, and private 5G to unlock new levels of urban intelligence, enabling data-driven decisions and automated operations across every city service.

  • Smart lighting reducing energy consumption by up to 70%
  • Intelligent traffic management reducing urban congestion
  • Real-time environmental monitoring for air and noise quality
  • Connected public safety infrastructure improving response times
Our Approach

A Connected Urban Brain for Every City Service

Our platform creates a unified data layer across all city systems — transport, utilities, public safety, waste, and environment — enabling city operators to see everything, understand patterns, and act proactively. From a single dashboard, city managers gain the intelligence needed to make better decisions faster.

  • Unified city operations center with real-time dashboards
  • AI-powered predictive analytics for urban planning
  • Open API architecture enabling third-party integration
  • Citizen-facing service portals and mobile applications
Our Solutions

Purpose-Built for Smart Cities

We deploy a suite of integrated IoT and connectivity solutions tailored to the unique demands of smart cities operations.

Smart Street Lighting

Adaptive LED street lighting that automatically adjusts brightness based on real-time conditions — pedestrian presence, traffic density, and ambient light — saving up to 70% on energy costs.

Traffic Management

AI-powered traffic signal optimization that analyses real-time flow data to reduce waiting times, prioritize emergency vehicles, and improve public transport punctuality across the city.

Environmental Monitoring

Dense networks of air quality, noise, and weather sensors provide real-time environmental intelligence — enabling data-driven responses to pollution events and informing urban planning decisions.

Public Safety IoT

Connected CCTV, gunshot detection, and public safety sensors that integrate with emergency services — reducing response times and improving situational awareness across the city.

Waste Management

Smart waste bins with fill-level sensors enable dynamic collection routing — reducing unnecessary collections by up to 40% while ensuring bins are never overflowing.

City Operations Center

A unified urban intelligence platform aggregating data from all city systems — providing city managers with the real-time insights and automated alerts needed for proactive city management.

Real-World Impact

Building Cities That Work Smarter for Everyone

Smart cities that deploy our platform deliver measurable improvements in livability, sustainability, and operational efficiency — while building the digital foundation for decades of future growth.
